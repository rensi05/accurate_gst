About Us

We Build Financial Fortresses, Not Just Filings

Accurate GST was founded on a simple idea: tax compliance should be a source of confidence for a business, not anxiety. Ten years on, that idea still drives everything we do.

Our Story

A Decade of Precision-First Advisory

Accurate GST started in 2014 as a two-person practice helping local traders navigate a brand-new tax regime. As GST law matured, so did we — growing into a full-service compliance partner for over 2,000 businesses, from first-time registrants to multi-state enterprises.

Today our team of certified GST practitioners, chartered accountants, and litigation specialists handles everything from routine monthly filings to high-stakes departmental audits — always with the same standard: zero-error accuracy and a straight answer, every time.
